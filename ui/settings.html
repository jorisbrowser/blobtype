Welcome to blobtype

Press a shortcut, speak, and your words are typed into whatever app you're using. Setup takes about a minute.

Choose a speech service

blobtype sends your recording to a speech-to-text service of your choice. You use your own account with them.

Connect

  1. Create a key on the provider's website.
  2. Copy the key and paste it here.
